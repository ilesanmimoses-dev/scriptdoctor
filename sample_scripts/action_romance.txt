FADE IN:

EXT. HARBOR DOCKS - DAWN

Mist rolls over the water. NINA RAMOS, thirty, runs along the pier with a camera bag on her shoulder.

INT. FISH MARKET - DAY

Crates crash to the floor. DANIEL OKAFOR, a tall man in a grey coat, pushes through the crowd.

NINA
Excuse me! That bag is mine.

DANIEL
Then you should hold it tighter.

He grins and runs. Nina chases him between the stalls.

EXT. ROOFTOP - SUNSET

Daniel jumps across a gap and lands hard. Nina follows without fear. They face each other, out of breath.

NINA
Who are you really?

DANIEL
A man who needs your photographs. They show the smugglers at the docks.

NINA
Why should I trust you?

DANIEL
Because they are chasing me too.

A black van screeches below. Men leap out and point up at the roof.

NINA
Then we run together.

DANIEL
I hoped you would say that.

INT. OLD WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

Nina and Daniel hide behind steel barrels. Gunfire rips through the wood. Daniel pulls her close and shields her head.

NINA
You are bleeding.

DANIEL
It is only a scratch. Look at me, Nina. Stay calm.

She presses her scarf against his arm. Their eyes meet. For a moment the noise fades.

NINA
I am not afraid anymore.

DANIEL
Good, because I think I am falling for you.

Nina laughs softly, then kisses him. The doors burst open. Police flood the warehouse and arrest the smugglers.

INT. POLICE STATION - MORNING

Detective ALVAREZ studies the photographs spread across his desk. Nina sits beside Daniel, wrapped in a blanket.

ALVAREZ
These pictures will put the whole gang in prison.

NINA
Then every bruise was worth it.

DANIEL
Not every one. I still owe you a camera lens.

ALVAREZ
Tomorrow the judge will hear everything. Get some rest.

EXT. HARBOR DOCKS - DAWN

Sunrise warms the water. Nina hands Daniel her camera. He smiles and takes her hand.

FADE OUT.
