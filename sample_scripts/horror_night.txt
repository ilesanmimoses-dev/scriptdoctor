FADE IN:

EXT. FARMHOUSE - NIGHT

Wind howls across the dead fields. EMMA, twenty-five, and her brother LUKE stand before a crooked house. One window glows red.

EMMA
Nobody has lived here for years.

LUKE
Then who lit the candle?

INT. HALLWAY - NIGHT

The door slams behind them. Cold air creeps along the floor. Shadows crawl up the walls.

EMMA
Luke, the door will not open.

LUKE
Stay close to me. I heard a whisper.

A scream echoes from below. They freeze.

INT. STAIRWELL - NIGHT

Emma finds a torn photograph pinned to the wall. Every face in it has been scratched away with a knife.

EMMA
Luke, look at this. They were a family once.

LUKE
Or a warning. Do not touch it.

A floorboard creaks above them. Dust drifts down from the ceiling like grey snow.

INT. CELLAR

A rusted staircase leads into darkness. Blood drips from the ceiling. Emma lifts a a lantern and steps down slowly.

LUKE
Emma, please. Let us go back.

EMMA
Something is down here. Listen.

A low growl rises from the corner. Claws scrape against the stone. The lantern flame shivers and nearly dies.

LUKE
It is behind you!

A pale figure drags itself from the shadows. Its eyes are black and empty. It reaches for Emma with a bony hand.

EMMA
Run, Luke! Run!

They stumble up the stairs. The figure screams and crawls after them, faster than any living thing.

INT. HALLWAY - NIGHT

Luke slams his shoulder into the door. The wood cracks. Behind them the whisper grows into a terrible laugh.

LUKE
Together, now!

They hit the door once more. It bursts open and they tumble into the cold night.

EXT. FARMHOUSE - NIGHT

Emma and Luke crawl across the dead grass. The red window goes dark. The house falls silent.

EMMA
Is it over?

Inside, a candle lights itself again.

FADE OUT.
