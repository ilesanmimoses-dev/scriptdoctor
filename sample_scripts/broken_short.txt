The night was dark and cold. A man stood outside the the old house and waited.

JOHN
Where are you going? You said you would stay with me. You said that this time everything would be different, that we would finally leave this town together and start again somewhere quiet, somewhere far from the noise and the people who always judged us. I packed my bags for you. I told my mother goodbye for you. I gave up the job I loved for you, and now you stand there and say nothing at all. Do you even remember the promise that you made to me under the old tree, the night before the rain came and washed everything away? Tell me the truth right now.

MARY

He   opened the door and looked at her for a long time.
and then he walked away without a word.

JON
It is over now. I will not come back.

The rain began to fall on the empty road.
